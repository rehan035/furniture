## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an editorial, tactile, and understated luxury design movement tailored for bespoke solid wood furniture craftsmanship. It merges high-end architectural publication sensibilities with the grounded warmth of natural hardwood, raw timber grains, and artisan joinery.

### Personality & Tone
- **Artisanal Authority:** Confident, time-honored, and master-crafted.
- **Warm Editorial:** Calm, spacious, literary, and evocative of high-end interior monographs.
- **Architectural Poise:** Clean lines, generous breathing room, structured framing, and disciplined visual rhythm.

### Emotional Response
Users should feel an immediate sense of quiet prestige, grounding warmth, unhurried deliberation, and uncompromised trust in bespoke craftsmanship.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from kiln-dried hardwoods, raw linen, honed stone, and aged architectural millwork.

- **Primary (`#4A3525` - Rich Walnut):** Used for commanding typography, primary interactive elements, and anchor branding surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#A06535` - Warm Amber Wood):** Serves as an artisan accent for micro-interactions, decorative dividers, and secondary focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#C28448` - Golden Ochre):** Applied sparsely for hover transitions, selected states, and specialized badges.
- **Neutral (`#FAF8F5` - Warm Ivory):** The structural canvas background, giving warmth without the stark sterility of pure white.
- **Named Colors:**
  - `walnut-deep` (`#2F2117`) and `charcoal-main` (`#1C1A17`): Reserved for high-contrast headlines, footer monoliths, and structural lines.
  - `surface-warm` (`#F5F1EB`) and `sand-border` (`#E8E2D8`): Used for card surfaces, architectural framing borders, and secondary module backgrounds.
  - `whatsapp-consult` (`#25D366`): Handled strictly in a muted luxury context (e.g., paired with warm ivory backgrounds or charcoal text) to maintain editorial restraint while offering direct artisan inquiry.

## Typography

The type system balances the literary heritage of furniture architecture with contemporary digital legibility.

- **Headlines:** Set in `Playfair Display`. Its high stroke contrast and sculptural serifs evoke exhibition catalogues and bespoke monographs. Display and Hero headlines should maintain tight, intentional line heights and negative letter-spacing for refined gravitas.
- **Body & Labels:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Its geometric proportion and crisp legibility counter the high-contrast serifs, keeping product descriptions, material specifications, and dimensions uncluttered.
- **Micro-Copy & Metadata:** `label-caps` must always be rendered in full uppercase with expanded letter spacing (`0.12em`) to function as architectural catalog indexers (e.g., "SPECIES: SHEESHAM / WALNUT FINISH").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored in a 12-column fixed-max-width editorial grid system (max container width: `1380px`), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column layout on mobile.

- **Whitespace Discipline:** Layouts lean into expansive breathing room. Modules are separated by deliberate pacing (`space-2xl` for desktop sections), reflecting gallery and showroom exhibitions.
- **Asymmetry & Framing:** Curated collections feature alternating column spans (e.g., 7 columns for architectural product photography paired with 5 columns of descriptive editorial text) rather than repetitive, rigid e-commerce grids.
- **Outer Canvas Margins:** On desktop, canvas margins span `4rem` to emphasize an open catalogue frame; on mobile devices, margins compress down to `1.25rem` while preserving internal element padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on synthetic drop shadows. Instead, it utilizes physical layering reminiscent of studio architectural modeling:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces layer naturally. Canvas base sits on `#FAF8F5`, elevated panels rest on `#F5F1EB`, and recessed or informational sections rely on `#E8E2D8`.
- **Architectural Ghost Outlines:** Cards, spec sheets, and containers are defined by razor-thin hairline borders (`1px solid #E8E2D8` or `1px solid #D8CEBE`) rather than floating drop shadows.
- **Tactile Ambient Shadows:** When elevation is interactive (such as a floating showroom concierge trigger or an active zoom modal), shadows are broad, ultra-diffused, and warm-tinted:
  - `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(47, 33, 23, 0.07)`
  - Hover states: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(47, 33, 23, 0.12)`

## Shapes

The design system adheres to a softly tailored architectural shape language (`roundedness: 1`).

- Corners have a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) easing, avoiding both the clinical edge of razor-sharp rectangles and the casual informality of pill/starkly rounded corners.
- Interactive buttons and inputs share this restrained 4px radius, preserving the feel of dressed, planed timber edges.
- Curated feature imagery may optionally use a single-axis arched top (`rounded-t-[8rem]`) for specialized editorial lookbooks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Walnut background (`#4A3525`), Warm Ivory text (`#FAF8F5`), 4px border radius. Hover shifts to `#2F2117` with subtle amber micro-underline transitions.
- **Secondary (Editorial Outline):** Transparent background, 1px border in `#4A3525`, text in `#4A3525`. Hover transitions to `#F5F1EB` background.
- **WhatsApp Concierge Button:** Deep charcoal border or soft ivory capsule containing a refined green insignia accent (`#25D366`), preserving luxury aesthetics without jarring standard neon blocks.

### Cards & Catalogue Frames
- Furniture showcase cards use `#F5F1EB` or transparent backgrounds, bounded by 1px `#E8E2D8` hairline borders.
- Image containers carry a subtle inset vignette or warm neutral baseline tint to ground furniture renderings and photos.
- Product tags ("Solid Walnut", "Made to Order") rest in uppercase `label-caps` in `#A06535`.

### Chips & Material Selectors
- Wood finish swatches (e.g., Sheesham, Walnut, Teak) are presented in 32px circular wood grain tokens bordered with a 1px `#E8E2D8` ring. Active states show a double-ring accent in `#4A3525`.
- Text chips use low-saturation `#F5F1EB` background with `#4A3525` typography and minimal 4px corners.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Clean architectural input fields with a baseline or thin perimeter border in `#D8CEBE`.
- On focus: shifts smoothly to `1.5px solid #4A3525` with zero glowing halos. Placeholder copy appears in muted walnut beige.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio controls feature a dual-ring walnut mechanism (`#4A3525` outer circle with an inner warm ivory air gap).
- Checkboxes use a squared 4px frame in `#4A3525` filled with rich walnut and a clean bone-white checkmark.

### Bespoke Showroom Additions
- **Dimension & Specification Strips:** Horizontal tabular grids displaying joinery type, moisture levels, wood species, and lead times with clean `1px solid #E8E2D8` dividers.
- **Material Comparison Drawer:** Side-by-side grain inspection panel using warm ivory surfaces, allowing comparison of fabric upholstery and timber stains.
